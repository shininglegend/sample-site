Scripture Graph

Visualizing intertextual connections in the New Testament

About This Project

This experimental visualization maps cross-references from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (TSK) as an interactive network graph. Each New Testament verse becomes a node, connected to the Old Testament passages it references or echoes.

Explore the web of biblical intertextuality—discover patterns, clusters, and the rich tapestry of connections between testaments.
