The following describes what the bot is designed to do, so you know what you are installing. No item below is guaranteed to exist, remain available, or behave in any particular way, and nothing in this section is a warranty or a commitment to provide a feature. See Disclaimer of warranties.

ShieldBot is intended to provide moderation and logging tooling for Discord servers, which may include:

  • Moderation actions — bans, kicks, timeouts, purges, and manual moderation log entries.
  • Audit logging — message deletions and edits, member joins and leaves, and moderation events, delivered to a channel or webhook of the server’s choosing.
  • Isolation and role restore — temporarily removing a member’s roles and restoring them later.
  • Automod filters — keyword filtering with configurable weights, exempt roles, and ignored channels.
  • Support tickets — an interactive ticketing system with claiming, closing, and optional feedback.
  • An optional AI assistant — off by default, subject to the additional terms below.
